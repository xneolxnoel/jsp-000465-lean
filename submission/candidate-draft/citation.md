Candidate review narrative (not an announced award).

JSP-000465 asks whether, for a finite family of graphs each containing a cycle, the asymptotic extremal number of the family is within a constant factor of that of some single member (corrected Erdős–Simonovits compactness). OpenAI’s *Ten Advances…* (2026), Chapter 10, Theorem 1.1, constructs a finite family `F` of connected bipartite cyclic graphs with `ex(n,F)=O(n^{4/3−1/48})` while every member still has `ex(n,F)=Ω(n^{4/3})`.

This candidate concerns a machine-checkable Lean 4 + Mathlib formalization of that theorem (`Compactness.theorem_1_1`).
